Yatmo Neighbourhood Map

Map, points of interest, travel times and an indexable neighbourhood text on your property pages

Yatmo gives real estate websites neighbourhood intelligence: schools, shops, public transport, healthcare and more around each property, with real travel times on foot, by bike, by car and by public transport, and a written summary of the area.

This module brings Yatmo to your Odoo website with two building blocks and two QWeb templates:

  • Yatmo Map: the interactive Yatmo widget (map, places, travel times, summary). Choose the layout and the look in the block options; the module builds the embed.
  • Yatmo Text: a written description of the neighbourhood (schools, shops, public transport, roads, leisure, nearby cities), written into the page as ordinary headings and paragraphs, so search engines and AI assistants read it as part of your listing.

A Yatmo licence key is required. Yatmo is a paid service for real estate portals, agency networks and developers. Contact us to get a key; the module displays nothing to visitors until a key is entered.

Building blocks

Drag the Yatmo Map block onto a page, type the property address (or its coordinates), and pick the layout: map with summary, map with Yatmo scores, map above the summary, map only, summary only, summary in tabs. Isochrones, routes, pin or discreet circle, colours and zoom are in the same panel. Everything left out follows the site defaults.

The Yatmo Text block writes the neighbourhood description into the page itself, styled by your theme: choose the paragraphs, the heading level, and whether the street and city names appear in the headings. The text exists in the languages of the property's country.

Pages generated from your records

For property pages rendered from your own model (or from products), call the templates from your QWeb views. Everything is rendered on the server, cached, and indexable: 

<t t-set="yatmo" t-value="{'latitude': property.latitude, 'longitude': property.longitude}"/>
<t t-call="yatmo_map.map"/>

<t t-set="yatmo" t-value="{'address': property.street + ', ' + property.city, 'paragraphs': 'education,shopping'}"/>
<t t-call="yatmo_map.text"/>

Python: env['yatmo.map'].render_map(values), render_text(values), build_params(values) and geocode(address, country, key).

Features

  • Site defaults in Website > Configuration > Settings > Yatmo: key, country, language, layout, look, modules, text.
  • Six layouts, discreet circle instead of a pin, isochrones (5, 10, 20 minutes), routes to any place the visitor clicks.
  • Favourite addresses: logged-in visitors save their home or office and see their own commute from every property.
  • Multilingual: 23 languages; by default the widget follows the language of the page.
  • Geocoding of addresses by Yatmo, cached for 90 days; the neighbourhood text is cached for 30 days.
  • Cookieless and lightweight: one lazy-loaded iframe for the map, plain HTML for the text.

Countries: Albania, Australia, Austria, Belgium, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Canada, Croatia, Cyprus, France, Germany, Greece, Ireland, Italy, Luxembourg, Malta, Montenegro, Morocco, the Netherlands, Portugal, Serbia, Slovenia, Spain, Switzerland and the United Kingdom.

External services

This module relies on Yatmo, a third-party service operated by Yatmo SRL, to display its content and to locate addresses.

  • Yatmo widget (https://map.yatmo.com/plugin.html): every page with a Yatmo Map block or template embeds this page in an iframe, with your licence key, the country, the language, the display options and the property coordinates. When favourite addresses are enabled, it also receives an anonymous identifier of the logged-in visitor (a hash of the Odoo user id) and the addresses that visitor saves.
  • Yatmo neighbourhood text (https://<country>.yatmo.com/Summary/text): for each property, your server sends the property coordinates and your licence key, and stores the returned text for 30 days.
  • Yatmo geocoder (https://<country>.yatmo.com/Geolocation): when a block or template is given an address instead of coordinates, your server sends that address, the country and your licence key once, and stores the result for 90 days.
